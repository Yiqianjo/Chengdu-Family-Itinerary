川西经典环线——先直奔九寨沟看水，再一路南下，穿羌寨、泡温泉、望雪山，到都江堰看两千年水利，顺路摸摸熊猫，最后回成都逛吃收尾。整条线从高原慢慢回到平原，越走越舒服。自然、人文、美食都有，节奏不赶。

Classic Western Sichuan loop—straight to Jiuzhaigou for the lakes, then south through Qiang villages, hot springs, and snow peaks, down to Dujiangyan's ancient dam, a visit with the pandas, and back to Chengdu to eat and unwind. The route descends from highlands to plains—easier as we go. Nature, culture, food, all at a relaxed pace.
